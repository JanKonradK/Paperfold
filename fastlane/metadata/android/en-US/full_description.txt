Paperfold is an e-book reader and note application. It keeps your books, your highlights, and your reading history on your own device.

Paperfold is a fork of Anx Reader by Anxcye. The upstream author does not endorse Paperfold.

<p><strong>Read</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Open EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, TXT, CBZ, and PDF files</li>
<li>Set the font, the size, the spacing, the margins, and the alignment</li>
<li>Choose a theme, a background, and a page turn style</li>
<li>Read left to right or right to left</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Take notes</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Highlight a passage and attach a note to it</li>
<li>Sort notes by time or by chapter, and filter them by color</li>
<li>Export notes to TXT, Markdown, or CSV</li>
<li>Share a passage as a card</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Track your reading</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>See how long you read each day, week, month, and year</li>
<li>See your reading habit as a heatmap</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Keep your data</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Sync books, notes, and progress through your own WebDAV server</li>
<li>Export and import your data</li>
<li>No account is necessary</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>What Paperfold does not do</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Paperfold has no AI functions</li>
<li>Paperfold has no text-to-speech</li>
<li>Paperfold has no in-app purchases and no advertisements</li>
</ul>
